## Owner's selected operating model

- **Paperless is an automation-first private administrative archive**, not a cloud drive or a repository for all PDFs. Archive final invoices, tax records, bank and insurance correspondence, signed contracts, government notices, warranties and high-evidence documents. Retain a lean taxonomy and avoid manual overhead.
- **Google Drive is the project, work, media and reference store**, including editable originals, price comparisons, quotations, planning and ongoing project materials. Some accepted quotes may justifiably exist both in Paperless (contract evidence) and Drive (future comparison).
- Archive **document by purpose**, not extension. Avoid treating draft offers, prior negotiations, photos or spreadsheets as mandatory Paperless imports.
- **Keep originals** during migration. Temporary Google Drive copies are deliberately retained until exact archived-document reconciliation and satisfactory *current* backup confidence, then get **explicit owner approval** before any actual deletion. No automatic deletion or mass migrations.
