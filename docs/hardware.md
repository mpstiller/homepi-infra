# Hardware

## Server

- Raspberry Pi 5, 4 GB RAM.
- db-tronic PCIe M.2 NVMe set/case with active cooling.
- Samsung 990 EVO Plus 2 TB NVMe.
- Official-class 27 W PSU from kit.
- 64 GB microSD retained as an old recovery clone; not normally inserted.

## Relevant household devices

- Sony KD-65AF9 OLED / Android TV.
- Sonos Era 300; Sonos One exists but was not always connected during setup.
- Yamaha R-N1000A planned for later integration.
- Google Nest Hub Gen 2 (Matter/Thread capable) and Gen 1.
- Google Nest Doorbell Battery.
- Nanoleaf Essentials bulbs.
- IKEA TIMMERFLOTTE sensor.
- 2x Eve Energy smart plugs.

## Storage stance

No 12 TB HDD has been purchased. This is intentional; real usage of the 2 TB NVMe should be measured first.
